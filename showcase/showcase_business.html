Global Compliance Framework

Built-in support for the world's major data protection regulations.

🇪🇺 GDPR

EU General Data Protection Regulation

  • ✓Right to access (automated data export)
  • ✓Right to erasure (automated deletion + audit)
  • ✓Data portability (JSON/CSV export)
  • ✓Consent management & tracking
  • ✓Data processing audit trails

🇺🇸 CCPA

California Consumer Privacy Act

  • ✓Right to know (data inventory)
  • ✓Right to delete (cascading erasure)
  • ✓Opt-out of data sale
  • ✓Non-discrimination guarantee
  • ✓45-day response tracking
