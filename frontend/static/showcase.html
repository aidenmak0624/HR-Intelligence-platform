Under the Hood

Multi-Agent Architecture: How It Actually Works

Every employee question flows through a 5-stage AI pipeline. This isn't a keyword-matching FAQ bot — it's a reasoning system that plans, retrieves, validates, and responds.

Each stage enforces security, compliance, and quality before the response reaches the employee.
The Router Agent classifies intent and dispatches to the right specialist. Multi-intent queries fan out to multiple agents in parallel.
Agents don't just answer — they reason, self-evaluate, and retry if confidence is too low. This prevents hallucinations and low-quality responses.
5-layer architecture: Clients → Middleware (security) → AI Core (agents + RAG) → Data (DB + HRIS) → DevOps
